About Visava Garden

Visava Garden is a fun-packed garden located in Vishnu Nagar, Nanded, Maharashtra, situated approximately 5.2 km away from the city center of Nanded.

Being the only major big garden in the city, it functions beyond a regular park by incorporating features of an amusement park. Contact info: 090110 00950, Website: www.nwcmc.gov.in.

Attractions & Features

Jurassic Park Theme

The core center of attraction is its unique dinosaur-themed garden featuring concrete statues of various dinosaurs.

Rides & Play Areas

Laden with big and small rides for children, teenagers, and families, including a train ride, merry-go-rounds, swings, and slides.

Fitness Tracks

Equipped with dedicated jogging and walking tracks alongside lush green play spaces that attract regular fitness enthusiasts.

Food & Facilities

Includes basic public bathroom facilities, wheelchair accessibility for seniors and disabled visitors, and affordable snack/fast food stalls.

Visitor Guidelines

  • Duration: Visitors typically spend around 1 to 2 hours enjoying the serene, clean, and well-maintained atmosphere.
  • Best Time to Visit: Recommended during evening hours from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM when locals frequent for evening walks and exercises.
