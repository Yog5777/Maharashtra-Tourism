About Sitabuldi Fort

Sitabuldi Fort is a historic hill fortress situated in the heart of Nagpur city, Maharashtra. Built on two hillocks — Badi Tekri (Big Hill) and Choti Tekri (Small Hill) — this mega-built fort played a pivotal role in the 3rd Anglo-Maratha War. It holds significant historical value in the evolution of trade, commerce, and defense of the Nagpur district.

Architecture

The initial foundation of Sitabuldi Fort was laid by the British East India Company in the 18th century and was later reconstructed by Mudhoji II Bhonsle. Built primarily of sandstone, granite, and laterite, the fort incorporates classic medieval defensive architecture featuring strong fortress walls, turrets for cannons, and strategic lookout gaps.

Inside the complex sits a two-storied elegant palace with long pillared corridors, spacious rooms, an Islamic-style mosque, and historical tombs commemorating fallen soldiers and Mysore Nawabs from the Anglo-Maratha wars.

History & Battle of Sitabuldi

The fort is most famous for the fierce Battle of Sitabuldi fought in November 1817 between the forces of Appa Saheb Bhonsle of Nagpur and the British East India Company. It witnessed crucial chapters of Indian history, including the imprisonment of Mahatma Gandhi during the freedom struggle.

Following the 1857 Indian Rebellion, Nawab Kadar Ali (grandson of Tipu Sultan) along with eight freedom fighters were executed on the ramparts of this fort. A historic mosque and memorials are maintained in their honor.

Current Status & Visitor Info

Sitabuldi Fort is currently home to the Indian Army's 118th Infantry Battalion (Territorial Army) Grenadiers. Maintained under strict Army administration as an eco-friendly zone, it remains one of the few forts continuously occupied and preserved since its construction.

Public Entry Notice: The fort is open to general public viewing on specific days of the year:
  • 26th January (Republic Day)
  • 1st May (Maharashtra Day)
  • 15th August (Independence Day)

Location & Map

Situated in central Sitabuldi, Nagpur, the fort is easily accessible from Nagpur Railway Station and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport.
