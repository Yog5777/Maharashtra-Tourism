Nagpur is a large city in the central Indian state of Maharashtra. Known as the "Orange City," it holds profound historical and cultural significance. The 19th-century Nagpur Central Museum displays items found locally, including fossils, sarcophagi, and Mughal weaponry. The Raman Science Centre has hands-on exhibits and a planetarium, ideal for visitors of all ages. Sitabuldi Fort, set atop the Sitabuldi Hills, was the site of the historic 1817 battle. To the southwest, the immense, domed Deekshabhoomi stands as a prominent Buddhist monument and pilgrimage site, symbolizing peace and equality.
