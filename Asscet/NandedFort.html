About Nanded Fort

Nanded Fort, also known as Nandgiri Fort, is located 4 km away on the banks of the Godavari River from Nanded city. Nanded is well-connected by rail, air, and road to major cities in India, and the fort is approximately 3 km (1.9 mi) from the Nanded Railway Station.

Historical Timeline

Ancient Era

Historical records show that the Nanda and Satavahana dynasties ruled Nanded, which was referred to as Nanditat in copper plates found in Vashim (Vatsagulma).

Sultanates & Mughals

Said to have been built by Muhammad bin Tughluq (1318), the fort subsequently passed through the Bahmani Sultanate, Gond chiefs, Ahmad Shah I Wali, Nizam Shah, and the Mughal Empire under Shah Jahan.

Maratha & Nizam Rule

Chhatrapati Shivaji gained control in 1670 before Mughal reoccupation. Later, it remained under the Nizams of Hyderabad until the princely state was annexed to the Union of India in 1948.

Geography & Architecture

Originally featuring a double fortification with 24 bastions (18-20 meters high), 8 bastions remain in good condition today. The fort area now features a garden and a water tank.

Visitor Information

  • Accessibility: The fort is accessible in all seasons, taking about 20 minutes to reach the entrance from the main city.
  • Alternative Route: Another route exists from Kinwat, though it passes through dense jungles.
  • Tour Duration: It takes approximately one hour to explore and visit the entire fort complex.
