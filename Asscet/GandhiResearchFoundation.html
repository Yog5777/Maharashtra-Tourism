About Gandhi Research Foundation

'Gandhi Teerth' is an international center for Gandhian study, research, and dialogue, initiated and promoted by the Gandhi Research Foundation. Its core objective is to preserve and promote Gandhian ideology and legacy for posterity.

Situated at Jain Hills in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, Gandhi Teerth is a magnificent 65,000 sq. ft. sustainable structure. Constructed under green norms designed to endure for centuries, the complex is nestled amidst 300 acres of lush, afforested hilly terrain. It is surrounded by sprawling lawns, mango orchards, serene walkways, and mounded sit-outs, providing a completely noise-free and pollution-free environment with residential facilities for researchers and guests.

History & Institutional Highlights

Inception & Inauguration

Inaugurated on March 25, 2012, by the President of India, Pratibha Patil. Sponsored by Jain Irrigation and the Bhavarlal Kantabai Foundation.

Comprehensive Complex

Houses the Gandhi Museum, Gandhi International Research Institute, archives, publication division, and rural development programs.

Rich Archives & Collections

Preserves 7,350 books, 4,090 periodicals, 4,019 photographs, 75 films, and 148 original audio recordings of Mahatma Gandhi's speeches.

Global Philately Collection

Features an extensive and rare collection of Mahatma Gandhi commemorative philately items gathered from over 114 countries.
