About Ballarshah Fort

Ballarpur Fort (earlier called Ballarshah Fort) is an ancient fort in the Chandrapur district of Maharashtra, located in Ballarpur city on the eastern banks of the Wardha River.

History & Origins

Gond Dynasty

Founded by Gond King Khandkya Ballal Sah (1437–62), who succeeded Ser Sah. He also founded Chandrapur city after discovering a miraculous healing pond named Akaleshwar Tirth.

Seat of Kingdom

For many years, Ballarpur served as the capital seat of the kingdom before Chandrapur city was established. The last Gond king, Nilkantha Sah, died in imprisonment here.

Architectural Features

Strong Construction

Built from large black stones forming a rectangular land fort layout with high walls, towers, and main entrance facing east.

Unique Design & Tunnels

Features two intact right-angled doors, a small riverside entrance, and undiscovered tunnels running through the fort walls.

Visitor Information

  • Nearest Airport: Sonegaon Airport
  • Nearest Railway Station: Chandrapur Railway Station
