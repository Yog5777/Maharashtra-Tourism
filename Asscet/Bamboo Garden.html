About Bamboo Garden

Sprawled across 49 acres on Old Bypass Road (Vita Bhatti Road, Laghuvetan Colony) in Amravati, Bamboo Garden was established in 2017 by the forest department. It stands as India's first unique bamboo garden with a core motto to educate visitors on the importance and diverse species of bamboo.

Virtually everything inside—from the entrance gate and bridges to huts and caves—is constructed using sustainable bamboo. Adorned by two scenic lakes (Wadali Lake and Futka Lake), the garden also features a magnificent cactus garden, lotus garden, children's park, and a women-run canteen serving local delicacies.

Key Attractions & Highlights

Vast Species Diversity

Home to over 63 bamboo species and 300+ varieties of cacti in the dedicated cactus garden.

Photography & Pre-Weddings

A top-rated favorite spot for pre-wedding shoots, scenic walks, and nature photography.

Adventure & Amusement

Features adventure sports like a 20ft high sky-wall, rope walk, horse riding, toy train, and kid-friendly rides.

Lake Views & Bird Watching

Adjoining Wadali and Futka lakes provide serene boating experiences and year-round bird-watching points.

How to Reach

  • By Train: Badnera Junction Station is the nearest railway station, located about 15 km away.
  • By Road: Well connected via regular state transport buses, local taxis, and auto-rickshaws from anywhere in Amravati.

Timings & Entry Details

  • Closed On: Mondays (Open Tuesday to Sunday).
  • Timings: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Best recommended visiting hours: 9:00 AM or 4:00 PM).
  • Entry Fee: INR 20 per person.
  • Activity Fees: Horse riding (INR 30), Toy train (INR 30 for kids / INR 50 for adults), Adventure rope walk (INR 10).
