Ambazari Lake Nagpur

Situated in Nagpur, along its southwest border is the Ambazari Lake. It is one of the largest among the 11 lakes in Nagpur City. This lake is the prime source of water that is supplied to the officials of the Government and some important people. This lake is the source of the Nag River. This being a prime important place in the Nagpur city, it is taken care of and maintained by the Municipal Corporation of Nagpur.

HISTORY OF THE AMBAZARI LAKE

The Ambazari Lake was built in the year 1870, under Bhonsle rule. The primary purpose of the lake was to supply water to the government officials and eminent people of the city through clay pipes. The Ambazari Garden that is located beside the lake was established in 1958. The name Ambazari is given to it, as it is surrounded by mango trees (Amba in Marathi).

GETTING THERE

Since the Ambazari Lake is situated on the western side of the Nagpur City, you can arrive at this destination with the help of any public transport.

THINGS TO DO AND SEE IN AND AROUND

Apart from enjoying the facilities of boating, games and rides, tourists can also visit local restaurants and nearby markets like Lila Vista Shopping Center and Purushottam Bazaar.

Ambazari Garden at Ambazari Lake

Spread over 18 acres of land, the Ambazari Garden is a much loved picturesque tourist spot. The highlight of the garden is the musical fountain with superb lighting effects.

How To Reach Ambazari Lake

The Ambazari Lake is located just 4 kilometres away from Nagpur Airport and 5 kilometres from the Railway Station.
