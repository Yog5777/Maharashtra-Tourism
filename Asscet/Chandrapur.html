Chandrapur (earlier known as Chanda) is a historic fort city located in the eastern region of Maharashtra. Founded by Gond King Khandkya Ballal Sah in the 13th century, the city sits near the confluence of the Erai and Zarpat rivers. Due to its rich natural coal reserves and thermal power generation, Chandrapur is famously known as the "Black Gold City". It is globally renowned for the Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve and ancient architectural landmarks like Ballarshah Fort and Mahakali Temple.
