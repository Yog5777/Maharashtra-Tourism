About Gangapur Dam

Constructed in 1954 in Gangawadi village (just 10 km away from Nashik), Gangapur Dam is a famous earth-filled dam built on the River Godavari and is notably the longest earthen dam in Asia. It was the first dam built in the Nashik district.

Built using clay, stones, mud, and sand materials, the dam features a catchment area of 357.4 sq. km, a maximum height of 36.57 meters, and can discharge 81,013 cusecs (2,294 cumecs) of water through its 9 radial gates. It plays a critical role in supporting river navigation, water supply, waste management, ecological balance, and accumulating water from the sacred Godavari River.

Highlights & Activities

Sula Vineyards

Located close to the dam, allowing visitors to easily combine wine sampling and a relaxing tour.

Boating Club

A newly inaugurated boating club acts as an added attraction, drawing more visitors to the dam.

Gardens & Picnics

Features a garden nearby (requires chief executive engineer's permission) serving as a lovely picnic spot.

Scenic Views & Wildlife

Spot migratory birds during the evening, watch the moonrise from the top, or explore surrounding vegetable orchards.

How to Reach

  • By Road: Buses and local transport ply directly to the destination from Nashik.
  • By Private Vehicle: Easily accessible by car since it is located just 10 km from the city in Gangawadi village.

Travel Tips & Best Time to Visit

Best Time to Visit: Recommended between October to March.

Travel Tips: Carry a camera to capture the spectacular drawing-book landscapes. Note that prior permission from the chief executive engineer of the dam is required to visit certain areas like the nearby garden.
