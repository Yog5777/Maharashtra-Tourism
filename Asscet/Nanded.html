Nanded is one of the most prominent historical and religious cities in the Marathwada region of Maharashtra. Situated gracefully on the north bank of the Godavari River, it is globally famous for Takhat Sachkhand Sri Hazur Abchalnagar Sahib Gurudwara—one of the five takhts in Sikhism. Formerly known as 'Nanditat', Nanded boasts a rich ancient heritage with references dating back to Puranic literature, the Nanda Dynasty, and the Lilacharitra treatise.
