About Suraj Water Park

Suraj Water Park, famously known as 'The Pride of Maharashtra', is an excellent water recreation paradise spreading over 11 acres. Created by Muchhala Magic Land Pvt. Ltd. and designed by White Water West Industries of Canada, it is a rare combination of modern technology and traditional values.

It holds prestigious records, including being a 6-time winner in the Limca Book of Records and a winner of multiple national and international awards. It features Asia's largest man-made fiberglass cave measuring 103 ft in length, beautifully decorated with mermaids, statues of Lord Shiva, and Natraj.

Rides & Attractions

Dhadkan Sabke Dil Ki

A pink-colored twin-tube slide simulating a waterfall journey with steep drops and cascades.

Ding Dong Sing Song

Light blue slides that swirl multiple times before dropping riders into a common pool.

Labak Zabak Matak Slide

Adventurous tunnel slides featuring a 100 ft dive stretching over 350 ft length.

Rainbow Slides

Colorful open-roof and tunnel slides with loops and turns for an extra adventure dose.

Rim-Zim Baarish Hall

A nightclub-ambience rain dance floor with high-octane music and sprinkler artificial rain.

Wave Pool

Simulates sea waves using artificial air blowing into the water for relaxation and family fun.

How to Reach Suraj Water Park

  • By Air: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International or Domestic Airport is about 30 km away, taking around 1 hour to reach.
  • By Train: Thane Railway Station (Central Line) is the closest station, located just 10 km away (approx. 15 minutes commute).
  • By Bus: Waghbil Naka Bus Stop is the nearest stop, easily accessible via B.E.S.T or local state buses.
  • By Car: Accessible via pre-booked cabs, self-drive vehicles, or personal cars along smooth roadways.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Visit the dry village-themed park 'Surajgadh' if you want to relax after water activities.
  • Carry waterproof sunblock cream with a good SPF rating.
  • Keep a basic first-aid kit, extra pair of clothes, a towel, and spare shoes handy.
  • Stay hydrated throughout the day to avoid fatigue.
