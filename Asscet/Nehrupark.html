Nehru Park Akola

Nehru Park is a serene and well-maintained public green space situated right in the middle of Akola city. It serves as a popular recreational spot for morning walkers, fitness enthusiasts, and families looking for a peaceful retreat.

PARK ENVIRONMENT & ATTRACTIONS

Featuring lush green lawns, shaded walkways, and colorful flowering plants, the park provides a refreshing escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. It includes dedicated spaces and play areas, making it a favorite destination for children and visitors of all ages.

GETTING THERE

Being centrally located within Akola, Nehru Park is easily accessible via local autos, taxis, and public transport options from any corner of the city.

THINGS TO DO AND SEE IN AND AROUND

Visitors can enjoy a calm morning walk, relax under the shade of trees, spend quality time with family during pleasant evenings, or explore nearby urban hubs and markets in Akola.

How To Reach Nehru Park

The park is located within easy reach of the main city areas and is conveniently connected to major transit points like the Akola railway station.
