About Wadali Talao

Tucked amidst mesmerizing nature, Wadali Talao is a popular reservoir sprawled on Chandur Railway Road in Amravati. Originally built to supply fresh water to city residents, it now serves as a refreshing getaway offering a peaceful environment set away from the city's hustle and bustle.

Located just 3 km away from the city center, it is an ideal picnic spot for families and friends, easily accessible via local transport.

What Wadali Lake is Famous For

Boating & Water Sports

Delve into exhilarating pedal boat and motor boat experiences operated by trained professionals.

Nearby Zoo & Garden

Features a nearby zoo and garden offering kids' rides, horse riding, a toy train, and special 3D rides.

Adventure Sports Area

Equipped with river crossing, high course plank walks, and balance-testing planks for young adventurers.

Peaceful Ambiance

A favorite hotspot for nature lovers looking to relax and unwind in scenic surroundings.

Visitor Information & Best Time

  • Best Season: July to February (Monsoon and Winters) when the reservoir is lush and the weather is pleasant.
  • Timings: Open on all days from 10:00 AM to 7:30 PM.
  • Entry Fee: INR 20 for adults and INR 10 for children (Special 3D ride is INR 30 per person).
