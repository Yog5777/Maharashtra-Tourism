About Upvan Lake

Built in the year 1880 by the Thane Municipal Corporation, Upvan Lake is an artificial yet eco-friendly water body situated in Thane, Maharashtra. Re-constructed by JK Singhania to supply water to the Raymond Factory, it is framed by the scenic Yeoor Hills as its backdrop.

Today, it stands as a popular spot for evening walks, family picnics, and major cultural events like hosting the prestigious Sanskriti Arts Festival. Visitors can also spot a grand statue of Lord Shiva in the middle of the lake, an illuminated fountain, an adjacent Ganesha temple, and a vibrant lakeside restaurant.

Activities To Do At the Lake

Boating

Enjoy two-seater and four-seater paddle boats or motorboats during evenings (Paddle boat ride: ₹50 for 30 mins).

Walking & Jogging

Dedicated jogging tracks attract fitness enthusiasts during morning and evening hours for a refreshing workout.

Picnics & Snacks

Perfect for Sunday family outings with numerous local eateries offering everything from chaats to pizzas.

Sunset Views

An ideal spot to stay back till dusk and experience breathtaking views of the sunset over the water.

How to Reach Upvan Lake

  • By Rail: Located about 5.4 km from Thane Railway Station. Cabs or auto-rickshaws are readily available (Auto fare approx. ₹60).
  • By Bus: Take a bus from Thane Station (West) to Upvan Bus Stop or Palaydevi Mandir Bus Stop (One-way fare approx. ₹15).
  • By Private Transport: Visitors can drive down directly, with ample parking space available for private vehicles.

Best Time to Visit

While temperatures vary from 22°C to 36°C in summers and down to 12°C in winters, the monsoon season is the best time to visit Upvan Lake. The surrounding trees and lush greenery come alive, offering a breathtaking and pleasant natural atmosphere.
