About Chatri Talao

Situated in the southeastern part of Amravati, Chatri Talao is a heritage water reservoir built in 1888 across a small spring called 'Kali Nadi'. Originally constructed as the primary drinking water source for the city during the British era, it currently gets stocked with water supplied from the Upper Wardha Dam.

The name "Chatri Talao" translates to "Umbrella water tank". Today, it functions as a serene tourist spot renowned for its peaceful natural landscape, black ducks, and recreational gardens.

Key Attractions & Highlights

Pakshi Jatra (Bird Festival)

A major draw from October to March when numerous migratory birds flock to the lake, making it paradise for bird watchers.

Boating Facility

Enjoy relaxing boating rides on the serene waters of the reservoir (priced around INR 50 - INR 100).

Garden & Picnic Area

Features a manicured garden filled with vibrant grasslands and colorful flowers, ideal for family outings and soul-nourishing walks.

Scenic Views

Offers gorgeous, picturesque sunrise and sunset views over the water tank.

How to Reach

  • Location: Located on Malkhed Railway Road, just 1 km from Dasturnagar Square and 4 km from main Amravati city.
  • Local Transit: Easily accessible via private cabs, auto-rickshaws, or even on foot from nearby squares.
  • Nearest Transport Hubs: Amravati Railway Station is the closest station; Nagpur Airport is located about 150 km away.

Visitor Information

  • Timings: Open on all days from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
  • Entry Fee: INR 10 per person.
  • Best Time to Visit: October to March (winter season) to witness the arrival of migratory birds.
