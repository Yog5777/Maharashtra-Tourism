About Gurudwara Nagina Ghat Sahib

Gurudwara Nagina Ghat Sahib is a historic gurudwara situated on the left bank of the Godavari River in Nanded, located about half a mile away from Takht Sachkhand Sahib.

The structure stands on a high plinth comprising a square hall where the Guru Granth Sahib is seated on a canopied palaki of white marble, and features a smaller square room topped by a dome on the first floor. It was initially constructed by Raja Gulab Singh Sethi of Delhi and completed by his widow in 1968.

History & Legend

The Nagina Legend

Tradition says Shri Guru Gobind Singh Ji sat by the river when a devotee presented a valuable Nagina (Pearl), which the Guru threw into the Godavari.

The River of Pearls

When the upset devotee was told to retrieve it, entering the river revealed it to be completely full of pearls all around, nullifying his false pride.
