About Tikuji-ni-Wadi

Tikuji-ni-Wadi is a water park and an amusement park located in Thane West. Spanning across an area surrounded by a chikoo orchard, it features Giant Slides, Wave Pool, Kiddies and Family Slides, Lazy River, Landscape Gardens, and an Aqua play shop. Designed along international safety standards, it offers great entertainment value for families and peer groups.

Founded over 30 years ago by Late Shri Pravinchandra A. Parekh as a peaceful resort getaway, it has grown into one of Mumbai's most popular tourist attractions recognized by the Tourism Department, Government of Maharashtra.

Entertainment, Rides & Timings

Amusement Park

Timing: 11:00 AM To 7:00 PM
Over 21 rides including Spin Coaster, UFO Cycle, Worm Coaster, Giant Wheel, and Animal Kingdom. Entry fee covers all rides.

Water Park

Timing: 10:00 AM To 6:00 PM
Features separate pools for kids and adults, wave pool, lazy river, and various water slides with lifeguards on duty.

Resort & Stay

Timing: 09:00 AM To 7:00 PM
Fully furnished AC cottages for longer stays, hosting corporate events, meetings, and weddings.

Food & Dining

Cafes and restaurants spread across the premises serving delicious vegetarian food and a treetop buffet lunch.

Things to Do at Tikuji-ni-Wadi

  • Amusement Rides: Enjoy dry rides like Dashing Cars, Go Kart, and family-friendly kid rides.
  • Water Games: Splash around in wave pools, lazy rivers, and safe kid zones.
  • Dinosaur World: Explore life-sized animated structures of pre-historic creatures like T-Rex and Spinosaurus.
  • Aquarium: Watch fishes living in their natural habitat and learn more about aquatic life.
  • Shivratri Festival: Famous for visiting during Mahashivratri to witness a massive ice-carved idol of Lord Shiva.

How to Get There

By Bus: TMT Bus Route No. 56 from Thane Station, MSRT from Western Suburbs (Borivali) to Manpada Village Junction and walk. BEST Bus route 700 or TMT route 65 from Borivali to Thane, get down at Manpada Village Junction.

By Car: Take the Eastern Express Highway from Sion Circle, pass Mulund Check Naka towards Kapurbawdi junction onto Borivali Ghodbunder Road, and turn left at Manpada village junction.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Keep your identity proof handy.
  • Follow age restrictions on adventure rides.
  • Wear synthetic or nylon clothes during water park activities.
  • Outside food and drinks are not permitted inside.
  • Swimming costumes are available for purchase at the park.
