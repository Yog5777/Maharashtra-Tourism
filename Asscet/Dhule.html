Dhule, formerly known as West Khandesh, is situated at the foot of the Satpuda hill range in northern Maharashtra. The Sahyadri mountain range reaches the western side of the district, alongside the scenic Galna mountains. Major rivers like Tapi, Panzara, Kan, Arunavati, and Aner flow through the region. Dhule is a well-connected hub via major national highways (Mumbai-Agra, Surat-Nagpur) and railways, featuring key tourist attractions like the historic Laling Fort and Ekvira Devi Temple.
