Akola is a major historical, commercial, and agricultural city located in the Vidarbha region of the central Indian state of Maharashtra. Situated on the banks of the Morna River, it serves as the administrative headquarters of Akola District and plays a vital role as a cotton and pulse trading hub.

The city and its surrounding areas are rich in heritage, featuring prominent landmarks such as the hill fortress of Narnala, ancient shrines like the Raj Rajeshwar Temple, and peaceful recreational spots like Nehru Park, making it an engaging destination for travelers seeking culture and history.
