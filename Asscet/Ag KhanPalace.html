Aga Khan Palace, Pune

Back to Pune

About Aga Khan Palace

The Aga Khan Palace was built by Sultan Muhammed Shah Aga Khan III in Pune, India. It was built as an act of charity to help the poor in neighboring areas of Pune affected by famine.

Declared a monument of national importance by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) in 2003, it holds deep historical significance tied to the Indian freedom movement.

Architecture & Specifications

Layout & Design

Features Italian arches, spacious lawns, five halls, and a signature 2.5-meter corridor wrapping around the entire building.

Construction Details

Spans across 19 acres with a 7-acre built-up area. It took 5 years and an estimated budget of ₹1.2 million to complete.

Freedom Movement & Legacy

  • Freedom Era Prison: Served as a prison for Mahatma Gandhi, his wife Kasturba Gandhi, secretary Mahadev Desai, and Sarojini Naidu.
  • Memorial Site: It is also the sacred place where Kasturba Gandhi and Mahadev Desai passed away during their imprisonment.
  • Museum & Society: Acts as the headquarters of the Gandhi National Memorial Society, housing archives, photographs, personal glimpses of freedom fighters, and a khadi shop.
