About Ekvira Mata Temple

Situated on the peaceful banks of the Panzara River in Dhule city, the Aadishakti Ekvira Devi Temple is a prominent and highly revered Hindu shrine. Located just 1.4 kilometers from the city center, it is easily reachable within 5 minutes by vehicle or a 15-minute walk.

The temple experiences a massive daily influx of devotees offering prayers. It comes alive during the Marathi calendar months of Chaitra and Ashwin, hosting grand traditional Yatras (fairs) and special rituals during Navratri and Chaitri Navaratri.

Heritage & Cultural Significance

Epic Mahabharata Legend

According to legend, Goddess Ekvira (an incarnation of Renuka Devi) appeared before the Pandavas during their forest exile (Aranyavasam) and promised them safety from discovery if they built her temple overnight.

Ancient Archaeological Roots

Carbon dating indicates the shrine's historical development spans across two primary eras: from the 2nd Century BC to 2nd Century AD, and later from the 5th Century AD to 10th Century AD.

Revered Family Deity

Deeply worshipped as a primary family deity (Kuldevi) by the Agri-Koli (fishermen) community, Daivadnya Brahmins, Kunbi, and numerous other communities across Maharashtra.

Grand Festive Fairs

Festivals like Navratri and Chaitri Navaratri draw massive crowds for special morning and evening pujas and vibrant annual fairs.

Visitor Information

  • Temple Timings: Open daily from 5:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
  • Special Rituals: Special aartis and pujas take place during early morning and early evening hours.
  • Local Transit: Easily accessible from the Dhule city center via private taxis, auto-rickshaws, and local buses.
