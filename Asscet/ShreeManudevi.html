About Shree Manudevi Temple

The district of Jalgaon in Maharashtra state is renowned for its magnificent cultural and religious places. Situated in the lush green ranges of Satpuda mountains, on the banks of River Tapi, is the Shree Manudevi Temple. It serves as the supreme family deity (Kuldevi) for many families residing in the vicinity.

History & Discovery

Discovery

The temple was discovered around 1251 A.D. by Shri K. Pandu Jeevan, a member of the Ingle family, through local excavations reflecting ancient culture.

Sacred Trees

Visitors are greeted by massive ancient trees. It is traditionally believed that Lord Parshurama manifests himself in these trees to welcome pilgrims.

Story of Shree Manudevi

According to the Devi Bhagwath Puranas, when Lord Vishnu, Lord Shiva, and Lord Brahma faced a severe menace from demons (rakshashas), the trinity of supreme deities met in a secret cave location within the Satpuda ranges—the present-day site of the Shree Manudevi temple.

Deep in meditation and prayer, a brilliant illumination of supreme energy (Shakti) appeared in the form of lightning, culminating into Goddess Manudevi, who represented the combined strength and intelligence of Brahma, Vishnu, and Mahesh. Upon the plea of the deities to eradicate the demon Mahishasur, the Goddess made a solemn promise to restore peace, battling through various historic locations like Shiragadh, Nandra (Bajara), Paatna, and finally the Sapthashrung mountains.

How to Reach & Trek Information

  • Road Route: Accessible via the Yaval Chopda State Highway, taking an exit and passing through Adgaon-Kasarkheda.
  • Terrain & Road Quality: Out of the final stretch, 75% is a well-paved two-lane motorable road, followed by 1.5 km of gravel road.
  • The Final Trek: The last 1.5 km involves an easy and scenic trek up to the temple situated at a height of 500 meters amidst lush green nature.
